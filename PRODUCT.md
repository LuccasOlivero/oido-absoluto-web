# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Supabase, Lucide React. Dual audio engine (YouTube IFrame API + HTML5 fallback).

## Users
Casual gamers, music history buffs, and trivia enthusiasts looking for a fun, addictive, and fast-paced musical challenge.

## Product Purpose
A high-stakes, fast-paced arcade game that challenges players to guess the exact release year of popular songs.

## Positioning
A high-risk/high-reward time-decoding interface combining music trivia with neon cyberpunk arcade aesthetics.

## Operating Context
Designed for quick, on-the-go mobile sessions and immersive desktop play.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Listen to a short snippet of a song (1s, 3s, or 5s).
- Guess the release year using a slider.
- Closer guesses yield more points and higher multipliers; exact guesses yield bonuses.
- Guessing further than 5 years off costs a life. Lose all 3 lives and it's game over.
- Shorter snippets offer massive score multipliers (Risk vs. Reward).

## Brand Commitments
- **Name:** TimePitch
- **Voice/Personality:** The Neon Cyberpunk Arcade. Dark, intense, neon-drenched, and highly technical. The UI resembles a sci-fi dashboard.

## Evidence on Hand
- Functional gameplay loop, Supabase leaderboard integration, YouTube audio streaming capabilities.

## Product Principles
1. **Risk vs. Reward:** Shorter snippets must clearly communicate higher point yields.
2. **Intense Feedback:** The game must respond with bright neon glows, digital readouts, and clear hit/miss states.
3. **Frictionless Onboarding:** Quick access to rules without blocking gameplay.
